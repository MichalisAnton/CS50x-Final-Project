Rules & Setup

Learn how to setup the quiz, how scoring works, and what to do during each round.

  • A player from each group creates a room, he then shares the link-code with the other players to join the designated room until leader closes registration

  • The game starts with the person who created the room being the leader and asking the first quiz question which appears on the screens of the players

  • Each Player submits their answers, and they appear on screen for each player to vote on

  • The winner of the round is announced and gains a point

  • Then, the leadership rotates in the order of joining the room
